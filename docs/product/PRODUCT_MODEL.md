# AgilePlus mature product model — specification under reconstruction

**Status:** the count-driven first catalogue is invalidated. See `governance/COUNT_INDEPENDENT_SPECIFICATION.md`.

AgilePlus is the repository-scoped governed specification and work-execution engine. It turns work intent into specifications, plans, work packages, claims, bounded execution, review, governance/evidence checks, shipping and attributable execution history.

AgilePlus owns working-change intent and work execution. Tracera owns accepted product intent, persistent product hierarchy, dissatisfaction and product-state interpretation. A completed work item does not automatically establish product acceptance.

The mature contract is defined first; bounded stages and work frontiers are derived afterward. Hierarchy, granularity and counts follow actual semantics. Do not impose fixed domains/features/FRs or duplicate shared constraints to inflate the functional inventory.

Early stages must be real usable forms with mature-shaped boundaries and bounded transition debt. Keep product shape, stage readiness, trace completeness, behavioral verification, external value and forecast survivability separate.

`spec/product/mature-contract.v1.json` is now an invalidation manifest. The generated AGP-MATURE-V1 is not an accepted baseline. Existing PRD, source-native FR/NFR definitions, ADRs and tests remain intact and must be reconciled. The replacement specification pass is incomplete.
